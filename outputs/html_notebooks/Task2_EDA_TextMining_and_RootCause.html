Task 2 - Automotive Complaint EDA, Text Mining and Root-Cause Analysis¶

This notebook implements the Task 2 brief end to end:

  1. Profile and clean the 1,000-record x 20-column warranty complaint dataset.
  2. Extract components, symptoms and error codes from the three VERBATIM fields with regex/keyword dictionaries, including Spanish normalisation and redacted-code detection.
  3. Assign one or more issue categories to every record.
  4. Cluster the complaints with TF-IDF + K-Means and interpret the clusters as root causes.
  5. Produce temporal and category-wise trend analyses and export the tagged dataset.

2. Text mining and entity tagging¶

The miner concatenates the three VERBATIM fields, normalises Unicode and Spanish terms, then applies component, symptom and error-code dictionaries plus issue-category scoring. Redacted numeric codes appear in the source as runs of spaces; the miner flags them and preserves the surrounding context instead of collapsing the whitespace.

3. Clustering¶

TF-IDF vectors (1-2 grams, sublinear TF) feed K-Means with a fixed random seed. The engine uses scikit-learn when it is importable and a NumPy TF-IDF/K-Means fallback otherwise. Empty clusters are reinitialised from the farthest point, and the top centroid terms expose each cluster's narrative.

6. Conclusion¶

  • The radio/display assembly and its communication path dominate every failure narrative.
  • Software/programming failures and electrical communication faults overlap heavily and should be treated as one platform-level workstream.
  • Redaction-aware mining recovered usable context for a quarter of the records despite removed codes.
  • Deliverables: this notebook, outputs/task2_tagged_complaints.csv, the cluster summary and the stakeholder Word report.
